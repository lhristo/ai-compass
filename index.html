ABOUT AI HRISTOV

A directory, not another chatbot.

Created by Lyudmil Hristov to help people discover AI tools that fit their needs.

Explore 62 AI tools and agent platforms in nine task-based categories. Search matches tool names, descriptions, task phrases and selected English/Bulgarian keywords. It may not understand every request or constraint. It does not send your task to an AI service.

Descriptions are editorial summaries of official product information, not independent benchmarks. Tools can have overlapping capabilities. Links open their official websites in a new tab.

We don’t use affiliate links, sponsored rankings, analytics or third-party tracking scripts. Saved tools stay in this browser’s local storage and do not sync across devices.

We do not process payments or promise free access. Check each provider’s current plans, regional availability, privacy terms and output licensing before using it. Only share material you’re permitted to upload.
